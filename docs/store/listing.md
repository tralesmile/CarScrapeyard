## Full description

Every empire starts with one rusty wreck.

Wash the mud off with your own two thumbs, tap to repair, haggle with the buyer and sell for cash. Hire a mechanic, a washer and a salesperson and the yard keeps working while you are away.

WHAT YOU DO
- Pressure-wash a wreck and discover what is hiding under the rust
- Repair it, haggle the price on the slider and sell it
- Crush hopeless cars for parts, restore a classic found in a barn
- Take jobs from the contracts board and finish daily tasks
- Collect 40 cars, from rust-bucket hatchbacks to hypercars, and earn medals

GROW A BUSINESS EMPIRE
- Repair Garage: customers bring a fault, you fix it and they pay
- Used Car Lot: win cars at the auction, refurbish and flip them
- Paint & Tuning: respray, rims, wraps and engine tunes, with tips for speed
- Move to Motor City, then sell your empire and start again with a legacy bonus
- Follow the story of Grandpa Joe's yard and your rival, Vince Valvano

PLAYS HOW YOU WANT
- Short sessions or long ones: your crew earns while you are away
- Works offline, no account needed
- Watch an optional ad for a bigger welcome-back payout or a 10-minute income boost; nothing is ever forced
- No loot boxes: auction lots use tokens you earn by playing

All cars, brands and people in the game are fictional.
