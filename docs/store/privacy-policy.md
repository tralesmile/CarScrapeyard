## What the game stores

Junkyard Empire keeps your progress (money, cars, settings) on your own device. The game works offline and needs no
account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

## What we collect

Today the game collects **nothing** and sends **nothing** to us. Analytics is built but switched off until you agree to it
on a consent screen. If you agree, we receive anonymous play events (for example "reached 50 sales") that carry no name,
email or device advertising id, so we can fix confusing parts of the game.

## Ads (phone app only)

If you choose to watch an optional rewarded ad, Google AdMob shows it. AdMob may collect your device's advertising id and
approximate location to show and measure ads. You decide in the consent screen (where the law requires one) and in your
device's advertising settings. The game works the same if you never watch an ad.

## Purchases (when added)

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store and RevenueCat. We receive an anonymous purchase record, never
your payment details.

## Cloud save (when added)

If you turn on cloud save, your progress is stored with Firebase under an anonymous id.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and is rated [age rating]. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Your rights

You can delete all game data by using Settings > Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app. To ask about data we may hold
(GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA), write to [email]. We answer within 30 days.
